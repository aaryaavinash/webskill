The idea / 00

A landing page can tell a story without saying everything at once.

Webskill is an instruction set for AI coding agents. It turns a loose request into an art direction, a sequence of visual scenes, an honest loading experience, and one interaction with a reason to exist.

01 / THE BRIEF

Find the feeling before the framework.

Choose a subject, a promise, and the world it should live in. Decide what the visitor sees first and why the next moment matters.

Direction → subject / palette / typography / asset
A world worth entering.
02 / THE SCENE

Give every scroll a point of view.

Map the page into distinct compositions. Let light, depth, text, and camera movement carry the story together instead of adding motion after the design is done.

Composition → scenes / camera / scroll / depth
SCENE 02 · FOCUSDEPTH / 0.78
03 / THE EXPERIENCE

Make the magic hold up.

Load what is real. Make the interaction discoverable. Keep the page useful on a phone, with a keyboard, with reduced motion, and when the 3D scene cannot run.

Delivery → loader / interaction / fallback / review
The standard / 04

Built to be experienced, not just screenshotted.
